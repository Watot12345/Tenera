# 🛡️ Tenera Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

*Effective Date: October 2026*

Tenera is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when using the Tenera Property Manager mobile application and website.

---

## 1. Offline-First Data Storage
Tenera is architected as an offline-first application. By default, all property listings, tenant contact details, payment receipts, and cash photo attachments are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database sandbox.

## 2. Cloud Sync & Firebase
When you choose to enable Firebase Cloud Sync:
- Sync is strictly opt-in and controlled by you under **More ➔ Cloud & Database Sync**.
- Records are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to Google Firebase Firestore servers.
- Data is partitioned under an anonymous unique landlord identifier generated on your device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your property records.

## 3. Camera & Storage Permissions
- **Camera / Photo Library**: Used exclusively if you choose to take or attach a photo proof of cash rent handover. Photos are stored locally within the application directory.
- **Network Access**: Used only to check for app updates via the public GitHub Releases API and to synchronize with Firebase when initiated.

## 4. Compliance
Tenera adheres to the principles of the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)** and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the [Tenera GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Watot12345/Tenera/issues).
