Tenera Property Manager v1.0.2

Android 7.0+ (API 24–36) · Offline SQLite SSOT · Startup & R8 Reliability Fix

Fixes & Reliability Improvements

  • Android Release Startup Crash Fix (AGP 9 / R8): Added explicit ProGuard/R8 keep rules for RoomDatabase and WorkManager reflective constructors so release APKs launch cleanly on all Android devices.
  • Instant Non-Blocking Launch: Moved Firebase, Trial verification, and Google Mobile Ads initialization to run asynchronously after the first Flutter frame so slow or offline devices never stall on startup.

Version v1.0.1 — Audit, SOA & Operations Update

Android 7.0+ · Offline SQLite SSOT

Ledger, Partial Payments & Security Deposit Escrow

  • Partial & Advance Payments: Record partial rent payments or advance credits with automatic running balance calculation.
  • Tenant Statement of Account (SOA): Full chronological ledger per tenant showing rent charges, utility splits, payments, and running balance.
  • Void & Adjustment Audit Trail: Mistaken payment entries can be voided with a mandatory reason while preserving immutable history.
  • Move-Out Deposit Settlement & Escrow Reports: Security deposits are tracked strictly as escrow liabilities with itemized deductions and refund calculation.

Tenant ID, Late Fees, Maintenance & Operations

  • Government ID Attachment & Late-Fee Rules: Attach tenant ID photos offline and configure optional grace days and late penalties with 1-tap waivers.
  • Expense Tracker & Maintenance Tickets: Log operating expenses per property and track unit repair tickets from reported to resolved.
  • Arrears Aging & SQLite Health Check: Bucket overdue balances by 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ days, and run on-demand SQLite PRAGMA integrity_check diagnostics.
  • Sub-Meter Utility Splitter & Lease Contracts: Compute exact electric/water sub-meter shares and generate printable Philippine lease contracts.
