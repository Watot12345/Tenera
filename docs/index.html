Landlord FAQ

Common questions

Clear answers on data privacy, offline reliability, and backups.

Does Tenera really work with zero internet?
Yes. Every property, room, tenant, payment receipt, utility split, and expense lives in a local SQLite database on your phone. You can collect rent and issue receipts completely offline.
Will Google Play Protect flag this APK?
No. Tenera removed invasive permissions like background SEND_SMS. Payment reminders open cleanly in your phone's default SMS or Messenger app so you stay in full control.
What happens if I change or lose my phone?
You can export an offline JSON backup anytime from More → Backup & Restore and save it to Google Drive or your computer. Business plans also include optional encrypted Firebase cloud sync.
Are security deposits counted as rent income?
Never. Tenera strictly separates security deposits into an escrow liability ledger so your monthly collected rent totals remain accurate for bookkeeping and BIR summaries.
Can my caretaker use the app without seeing my profits?
Yes. Enabling Caretaker / Staff PIN Mode hides financial totals and net revenue behind your landlord PIN while still letting caretakers check rooms and log tenant handovers.
