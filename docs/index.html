Roof Ledger Dashboard

Live monthly totals computed directly from SQLite. Instantly see collected rent vs. expected rent, overdue tenants, and vacant units.

📸

Cash Photo & Digital Proof

Take or attach photo proof when receiving paper cash, or log GCash and Bank Transfer reference numbers to prevent disputes.

🔍

Fast Tenant Search

Type any tenant name, room number, or initials in the payment dialog to instantly match and log payments in seconds.

🏢

Visual Room Grid

Color-coded status blocks (Occupied, Overdue, Vacant). Long-press any room or tenant assignment to remove or vacate tenants.

📴

100% Offline Single Source of Truth

All property listings and tenant ledgers are stored locally in SQLite (`tenera.db`). Works completely without Wi-Fi or cellular data.

Automatic In-App Updates

Tenera checks the GitHub Releases API and prompts you when a newer APK is available with changelog notes and one-tap download.

How to Install on Android

Follow these quick steps to install the APK directly on your Android phone or tablet.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download Tenera APK (v1.0) button above to download app-release.apk to your device.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

If your phone displays "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings and switch Allow from this source to ON.

3

Install & Open

Tap Install on the prompt. Once finished, tap Open to start managing your properties with Tenera!

4

Seamless In-App Updates

You never have to manually browse for new releases. Whenever a new version is released on GitHub, Tenera will automatically notify you inside the app!
