🛡️ Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective Date: November 2026 · Version 1.0.1

1. Offline-First Privacy Guarantee

Tenera is architected from the ground up as an offline-first application. By default, your property data, tenant personal information, government ID photo attachments, rental ledgers, expense logs, and cash handover photo proofs are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database sandbox (tenera.db).

2. Cloud Sync & Firebase

Cloud synchronization is 100% opt-in. When initiated under More ➔ Cloud & Database Sync, records are transferred securely using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption to Google Firebase servers under a private, device-partitioned landlord identifier. We never sell, rent, or monetize your property or tenant records.

3. Device Permissions & Play Protect Safety

Camera / Storage: Used strictly when you choose to take or attach a photo proof of cash rent handover or a tenant government ID. Photos stay inside local device storage.
SMS & Messaging: Tenera does not request invasive background SEND_SMS permissions. All SMS and Messenger payment reminders open safely in your phone's default messaging app so you always review and control every message sent.
Network & Advertising: Network access is used to check for APK updates via the public GitHub Releases API, synchronize with Firebase when manually triggered, and serve non-intrusive Google AdMob banners on the Starter plan (Tenera Pro and Business plans are 100% ad-free).

4. Data Protection Compliance

Tenera complies with the provisions of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Questions & Feedback

If you have any questions or feedback regarding privacy or data security, please open an issue on the Tenera GitHub Repository.
