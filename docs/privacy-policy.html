🛡️ Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Offline-First Privacy Guarantee

Tenera is built from the ground up as an offline-first application. By default, your property data, tenant personal information, rental ledgers, and cash handover photo attachments are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database sandbox.

2. Cloud Sync & Firebase

Cloud synchronization is 100% opt-in. When initiated under More ➔ Cloud & Database Sync, data is transferred securely using industry-standard TLS encryption to Google Firebase Firestore servers under a private, anonymous device identifier. We never sell, rent, or monetize your property records.

3. Device Permissions

Camera / Storage: Used only when you choose to take or attach a photo proof of cash rent handover. Photos stay in local storage.
Network: Used solely to check for APK updates via the public GitHub Releases API and to synchronize with Firebase when manually triggered.

4. Compliance

Tenera complies with the provisions of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Questions & Feedback

If you have any questions or feedback, please open an issue on the Tenera GitHub Repository.
